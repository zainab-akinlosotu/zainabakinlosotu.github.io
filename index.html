Offshore Oil Rig Maintenance Scheduling Optimization

Developed an optimization model for offshore oil rig maintenance scheduling using EIA and BSEE datasets. Applied Excel Solver to minimize unnecessary vessel dispatches while maintaining regulatory compliance, presenting findings at the Salisbury University Student Research Conference (SUSRC) in April 2026.

Key Result

Independent Research · Finance · Data Analysis

Data Breach Severity & Stock Price Impact Analysis

Conducted independent research under faculty mentor Prof. Gene Hahn analyzing how corporate data breaches affect stock prices. Examined 30 breaches across 18 years (2006–2024), challenging the conventional wisdom that breach severity drives negative market reactions. Also presented at SUSRC April 2026.

Key Finding
R² ≈ 0.00006 — breach size explains less than 0.01% of post-disclosure stock price variation. 60% of breached companies showed positive returns post-disclosure, upending common assumptions about market punishment.
